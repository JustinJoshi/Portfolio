## Why a Devlog?

I've always believed that the best way to learn is by doing -- and the best way to *retain* what you've learned is by writing it down. This devlog is my space to do exactly that.

As a software engineer, I'm constantly picking up new tools, running into interesting problems, and building things I'm proud of. But those lessons can fade fast if you don't capture them. So here we are.

## What to Expect

Here's what you'll find in this devlog:

- **Project breakdowns** -- deep dives into what I'm building and the decisions behind the code
- **Lessons learned** -- the bugs, the breakthroughs, and everything in between
- **Tech explorations** -- new frameworks, libraries, and patterns I'm experimenting with

## Let's Go

If you're a fellow developer, a recruiter checking out my work, or just someone curious about the craft -- welcome. I'm glad you're here.

Stay tuned for more posts. The journey is just getting started.
